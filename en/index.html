How we decide what to build

No program starts from a hunch. Before a single line of code, it goes through the same filter.

01

We find the complaint

2 and 3-star reviews, Reddit threads, industry forums. The real problem, not the one that sounds good in a meeting.

02

We measure pain and willingness to pay

How much the problem costs the person who has it, and whether there are real signs they'd pay to fix it.

03
