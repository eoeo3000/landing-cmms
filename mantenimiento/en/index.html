Questions before you join

What if my shop has no internet at all, ever?

Turno installs on the technician's phone or tablet and runs fully offline: creating, closing and documenting work orders needs no signal at all. Data syncs on its own once the device gets coverage again, even hours later.

How long until it actually works, not just "is installed"?

The goal is to have your assets and your first preventive plan loaded in a single working session, with guided support included. It's not a months-long rollout project.

Any fine print on the flat price?

No. The founding price includes unlimited technicians and assets. If your shop grows, you don't pay more for that.

Does the product exist yet, or is this a concept test?

We're validating the product with the first shops before public launch. Whoever joins now helps shape what gets built first, and keeps the founding price for as long as they're a customer.
